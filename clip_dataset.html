Common classes: within-class and close-class sets

For each class: a query image, the 20 images of the same class nearest to it by CLIP ViT-L/14 image similarity (within-class), and the 20 nearest images of other classes, at most one per class (close-class). Candidates were ranked by CLIP and checked to show the whole object, then curated by hand. ImageNet: the 50k validation images. Objaverse: the 43k LVIS-labelled objects, by their Sketchfab thumbnails. Click any image to enlarge.
