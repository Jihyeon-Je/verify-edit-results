Describing object classes

Thank you for helping with the survey! You will see the names of 10 classes. For each one, write up to 10 properties that describe it, ranked in order of importance (most important first). It should take about 15 minutes.

Properties should be visual and structural, and general to the class, not to one instance

Describe what members of the class look like: their parts, shapes, sizes, colors, and how the parts are arranged. Do not just say what the object is: for example, you cannot say "This is a dog."

Write what is true of members of the class in general, the way you would describe the class to someone who has never seen one. Try to think of the most general description of the class. Do not describe one particular example, such as its background, pose, or the color of one individual.

General A bicycle has two wheels, one behind the other.
General The pedals are between the two wheels, below the seat.
Instance The bicycle is leaning against a wall.
Instance Its frame is red.
Not visual This is a dog.

For each class there is a button that shows 5 example instances of the class. You may optionally refer to them, but please note that they are only there to help you: they are a small subset of the entire class, and there could be many other instances they do not cover.
